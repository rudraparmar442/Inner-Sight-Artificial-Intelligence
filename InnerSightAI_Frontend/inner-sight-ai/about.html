Under the hood

How Inner Sight AI thinks

01

Adaptive Question Flow

Unlike static questionnaires, every question you see depends on your previous answer. If you mention tension, we probe context. If you mention tiredness, we explore why.

02

Multi-Dimensional Mood Mapping

We don't just detect "sad" or "happy." We map valence (positive/negative), arousal (high/low energy), and context (situational vs. general). This creates a precise emotional fingerprint.

03

Real-Time NLP Engine

Your answers are processed through a language model that understands semantic meaning, not just keywords. "I feel like everything is too loud" maps to anxious — even though the word "anxious" never appears.

04

Solution Matching

Once your mood is mapped, our recommendation engine cross-references your specific profile with curated activities: breathing patterns, ambient soundscapes, journal prompts, or movement guides.
